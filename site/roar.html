HOW THE ROAR WORKS

Private ULTON test. Contract and burn addresses are excluded; project-wallet exclusions for $LOAD will be configured before launch. Points equal the current token balance multiplied by (1 + boost / 100). The original 100-position draw weighting is confirmed.

The page checks for a new published snapshot every five minutes. Snapshot age updates continuously. At the deadline the timer stops, while final results remain pending verification. Values remain fixed at the verified block shown above; this does not mean a new on-chain scan completes every five minutes. This test does not activate prizes.

The full 100-wallet model targets 60% of draw weighting for positions 1–25, 30% for 26–50 and 10% for 51–100. Displayed chances are the confirmed full 100-wallet model, not active prize eligibility. If fewer than 100 wallets qualify, draw weighting must be finalized before the draw.

Each event runs Monday 00:00 UTC to Sunday 23:59:59 UTC. Existing holders start accruing boost at the event opening. A new wallet starts on a verified DEX purchase; additional purchases preserve the wallet’s boost. Any sale or outgoing transfer resets the boost; a new purchase restarts it. All boost resets at the next event opening. New purchases count until the final second.
