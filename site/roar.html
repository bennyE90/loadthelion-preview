How the mission will work

  • Project-controlled wallets are excluded from the competition.
  • Automated, verifiable snapshots are planned, with updates every five minutes once the verified data service is connected.
  • The leaderboard will open with 10 wallets. SHOW MORE will reveal the next 10, up to the Top 100.
  • Wallet balances and holding-time calculations require complete on-chain history. Final scoring rules will be published before activation.
  • Winners will be verified by wallet-message signature and a second independent check. Verification will not require X.
  • NFT purchases and prize transfers will be handled manually. There are no automatic NFT transfers from this website.

Prize availability, eligibility, draw procedures and dates will be announced before the competition begins.
