THE MONTHLY RULES
  • The event runs from the first day at 00:00 UTC until the next month begins. Every wallet starts with zero monthly net buy.
  • Each operation uses its USD value when it happens. Later price changes do not rewrite past points.
  • Negative scores remain negative. Transfers between your wallets cannot create buying points.
  • Project wallets are excluded. The three highest eligible positive net buys receive the three prizes.
  • Ties favour the wallet that reached its final score first. An exact timestamp tie uses wallet address order.
  • The final ranking requires complete verified monthly history. Live rankings remain provisional until the final snapshot.
