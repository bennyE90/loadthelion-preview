HOW THE ROAR WORKS

The experimental ROAR BOOST ranking uses points = token balance × (1 + ROAR BOOST / 100). All eligible positive-balance wallets are included in the calculation, including holders outside the displayed Top 100. Project-controlled wallets are excluded. The first 10 positions are shown initially; SHOW MORE reveals 10 more at a time.

ROAR BOOST grows continuously from 0% to +200% over six days. Additional purchases preserve the boost; any sale or outgoing transfer resets it. After a reset caused by an outgoing movement, boost starts accruing again only after a new purchase. The weekly cycle restarts every Monday at 00:00 UTC (02:00 in Italy during summer, 01:00 during winter).

ROAR BOOST is an experimental specification in the test environment; it does not change the approved official V124 rules.

This historical ULTCROW snapshot contains balances only. It does not contain verified weekly movement history, so the preview does not calculate or display real ROAR BOOST points yet.

Loaded Lions NFT rewards, winner verification by wallet signature, and manual NFT transfer will be announced with the full official rules before launch.
